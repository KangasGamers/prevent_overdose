Scope: the PreventOverdose marketing site, homepage primary. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audiences: someone who needs naloxone now; a community member with time or money to give; a grant officer or prospective board member judging legitimacy in ninety seconds. Action: get a kit, take a training seat, give, or apply. Proof: real CDC/NCHS and CT OCME figures only — the org has no verified impact numbers of its own. Constraint: no fabricated statistics, board names, or photography; placeholders must read as placeholders.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Three audiences arrive in incompatible states, so the first viewport is a routing decision, not a hero statement. Refuses the single centered headline over a stock photograph that every nonprofit homepage ships.

OWN-WORLD: Brand red `#C4322B` carries the surface as full-height fields, not accents — a Committed color strategy against paper `#F7F6F2`, with blush `#EECECE` as fill only (it fails contrast for text) and ink `#222222`. League Spartan set very large, tight, and in caps; the tiled wordmark watermark is the red field's texture. Rules and hairlines divide; no shadows, no rounded card soup.

STORY: The visitor understands within one screen that this organization gives away the thing that reverses an overdose, believes it because real CT and national figures name naloxone as a cause of falling deaths, and takes the one door that matches their state.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Three floor-to-ceiling doors split the screen — I NEED NARCAN (red, widest, left), I WANT TO HELP (paper, center), WHO WE ARE (blush, right). Thin masthead above with wordmark left, nav and DONATE right. Watermark texture runs continuously beneath all three panels. Each door carries its label in caps at display scale, one line of consequence, and a drawn arrow that is the affordance; no numerals, since three parallel doors are not a sequence. Primary action is the red door itself, not a button inside it.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Doors respond to pointer proximity — the hovered panel widens and its neighbours yield, the watermark beneath shifting at a different rate, so the fork feels like a physical choice being weighed. Keyboard focus produces the same widening. Honors `prefers-reduced-motion` by swapping to an instant, non-animated state change.

FORM: The Triage Fork, index 1 of seven ranked structures, dealt as one of 4/1/7. Seed key f3d32b67, surface scope, persuade mode. Code-led build: no comp round.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

Unresolved: photography direction, board identities, and the org's own impact figures are all absent and render as labeled placeholders this phase.
